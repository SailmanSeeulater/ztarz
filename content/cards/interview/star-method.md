## Prompt

What does STAR stand for, roughly how should the time split, and what's a common mistake?

## Answer

- **Situation** (about 20%): just enough context to follow the story.
- **Task** (about 10%): your responsibility or goal.
- **Action** (about 60%): what *you* did and why. This is most of the answer.
- **Result** (about 10%): the outcome, measured if you can, and what you learned.

Common mistakes: saying "we" throughout, so your own part disappears; spending half the answer on the situation; and stopping without a result. Prepare three to five stories as outlines, not scripts, so they sound natural.
