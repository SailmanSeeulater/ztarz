## Prompt

"Any questions for us?" What makes a good question, and what are a few you'd ask?

## Answer

Always have two or three ready; having none reads as low interest. A good question can't be answered by the website, shows you're picturing doing the job, and helps you spot red flags. Fit it to the person: engineers for the work, managers for growth and the team.

- **The work:** "What's the hardest engineering problem the team has right now?" "What would I work on in my first three months?"
- **How the team works:** "How does a change get from pull request to production?" "What happens when something breaks at night?"
- **Growth:** "How do new engineers ramp up?" "What does doing well in this role look like after a year?"
- **Candour:** "What frustrates you most about working here?"
- **Close:** "What are the next steps?"
